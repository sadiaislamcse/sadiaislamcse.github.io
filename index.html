I'm an AI specialist with a foundation in machine learning, deep learning and NLP. My research on explainable ML for cervical cancer detection was published in the Journal of Pathology Informatics, and I work with TensorFlow, PyTorch, Keras, Scikit-learn and Hugging Face.
